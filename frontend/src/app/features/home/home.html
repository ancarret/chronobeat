How a round works

  1. 01

    Listen

    A short preview plays. The song's title, artist and year stay hidden.

  2. 02

    Place it

    Drop it into your timeline where you think it chronologically belongs.

  3. 03

    Reveal

    Get it right and it joins your timeline permanently, making the next round harder.

Play your way

Solo

Build the longest timeline you can before you run out of lives, or race to a goal.

Same device

Pass one phone around the table. Everyone builds their own timeline.

Online

Everyone joins from their own phone with a short room code and plays live.

Two ways to deal

Take turns

Each turn is one player's own song, placed on their own timeline. The classic party rhythm.

Same songs

Everyone hears the same song each round and places it at the same time. A fair race: first to 10 wins.
