How a round works

  1. 01

    Listen

    A short preview plays. The song's title, artist and year stay hidden.

  2. 02

    Place it

    Drop it into your timeline where you think it chronologically belongs.

  3. 03

    Reveal

    Get it right and it joins your timeline permanently, making the next round harder.

Two ways to play

Solo

Build the longest timeline you can before you run out of lives.

Local multiplayer

Pass the device around. Everyone builds their own timeline, same device, same songs.
